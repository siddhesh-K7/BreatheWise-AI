AirWise is an urban air quality assistant for severe winter smog. Built for Hack2skill PromptWars Challenge 6.

Key Capabilities:
- Live Open-Meteo telemetry (PM2.5, PM10, gases, boundary layer inversion) with WAQI physical station cross-check. Elevates safety AQI if variance > 50.
- NASA FIRMS satellite fire detection feeds stubble burning context into Google Gemini Flash.
- Gemini synthesizes schema-validated structured plans: diurnal routines avoiding peak inversions, cost-tiered indoor hacks (Free/Under INR 500/Over INR 500), and sustainable alternatives with quantified CO2 savings.
- Deterministic Berkeley Earth cigarette equivalence and CPCB transit emission math with real-time what-if simulator.
- Real-time streaming conversational AI advisor with prompt injection defense.
- Full WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility, zero violations via axe-core, keyboard navigation, English, Tamil, and Hindi localization.
- 100% serverless, zero client-side keys, zero PII persistence.
